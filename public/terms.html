1. About Flytte

Flytte (“Flytte”, “we”, “us”) provides apps that let riders request boda, car, parcel and airport trips from independent drivers in Uganda. Drivers are not Flytte employees; they choose when to drive and are responsible for their vehicles. By creating an account or using Flytte, you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 and give accurate details. One account per person.
  • You sign in with a one-time code sent to your phone. Keep your phone secure and never share your codes; you’re responsible for activity on your account.
  • We may suspend an account that breaks these Terms, puts others at risk, or is used for fraud.

3. Trips and prices

  • Before you book, Flytte shows an upfront price based on distance, expected time and demand. Prices may be higher at busy times or in rain; the price you accept is the price you pay.
  • The price may change if you change the trip, such as adding a stop, changing the destination, or long waits at pickup.
  • Tolls, parking or airport fees, where they apply, are added to the fare.
  • Each trip has a PIN. Share it only with your driver at pickup.

4. Payments

  • You can pay with cash, MTN or Airtel Mobile Money, card, or your Flytte Wallet, where available.
  • Cash is paid directly to the driver at the end of the trip. Other payments are processed by our payment providers.
  • Flytte Wallet balances can be used for trips. They are not a bank account, don’t earn interest, and can’t be exchanged for cash except where the law requires.
  • If something went wrong with a charge, contact us within 30 days and we’ll review it.

5. Cancellations and waiting

  • You can cancel a request before a driver arrives. Repeated late cancellations may limit your account.
  • Drivers wait a free period at pickup (shown in the app). If you don’t show up after that, a no-show fee may be charged from your Flytte Wallet and paid to the driver.

6. Conduct and safety

  • Treat everyone with respect. No violence, harassment, discrimination or illegal items.
  • Riders on bodas must wear the helmet provided; drivers must follow traffic laws.
  • If the driver or plate doesn’t match the app, don’t start the trip — report it in the app.
  • In an emergency, use SOS in the app and call 999.

7. Drivers

  • Drivers must hold a valid driving permit, keep their National ID, insurance and vehicle documents current, and use only the approved vehicle and number plate.
  • Profile photo, vehicle and plate changes need Flytte’s approval before riders see them.
  • Flytte keeps a commission from each fare (shown in the Driver app). For cash trips, the commission is owed to Flytte; cash requests may pause if the amount owed passes the limit shown in the app.
  • Earnings can be cashed out to Mobile Money, subject to the minimum shown in the app.
  • We may pause or end access for unsafe driving, fraud, repeated cancellations or confirmed mismatch reports.

8. Parcels

Don’t send illegal, dangerous, perishable or high-value items. Package items securely. The recipient confirms delivery with the code shown in the app. Report problems within 24 hours.

9. Offers and rewards

Discounts, invite rewards and Flytte Rewards points have no cash value, can’t be transferred, and may change or end. We may cancel rewards gained through abuse or fraud.

10. Liability

We work hard to keep Flytte available and safe, but we can’t guarantee the service will always be uninterrupted or that a driver will always be available. To the extent the law allows, Flytte isn’t liable for indirect losses. Nothing in these Terms limits rights you have under Ugandan consumer law or liability that can’t be excluded by law.

11. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Account → Settings → Delete my account. Any Flytte Wallet balance is lost when you delete your account, and drivers must cash out first. We may close accounts that break these Terms.

12. Law and disputes

These Terms are governed by the laws of Uganda. If you have a complaint, contact us first through Help in the app and we’ll try to resolve it. If we can’t, disputes go to the competent courts of Uganda.

We may update these Terms. If the changes are important, we’ll tell you in the app before they take effect.

1. Who we are

Flytte (“Flytte”, “we”, “us”) runs the Flytte rider app, the Flytte Driver app and related services in Uganda. We decide how your personal data is used, and we handle it in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda and its Regulations.

2. What we collect

Riders

  • Account details: your name and mobile number, and optionally an email address and an emergency contact.
  • Trip details: pickup and drop-off points, routes, times, fares, ratings, tips, messages with your driver, and saved places such as Home and Work.
  • Payments: the method you choose (cash, MTN or Airtel Mobile Money, card or Flytte Wallet), the Mobile Money number you pay from, and payment references. Card details are handled by our payment provider; we don’t see or store full card numbers.
  • Safety: SOS alerts and reports you make, with the location at the time.

Drivers — in addition to the above:

  • Identity and eligibility: National ID, driving permit, insurance sticker, profile photo taken in the app, vehicle details and photo, number plate and expiry dates.
  • Earnings and payouts: trips, earnings, commission, bonuses and the Mobile Money number for cash outs.
  • Activity: when you’re online, requests accepted or declined, and cancellations.

From your device: approximate and precise location (see section 5), device type, app version and diagnostic information needed to keep the service working.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including sending one-time sign-in codes.
  • To match riders with nearby drivers, show live trip progress and calculate fares.
  • To take payments, pay drivers and keep accurate financial records.
  • To keep people safe: verify drivers, check that a driver matches their photo and plate, respond to SOS alerts and reports, and prevent fraud.
  • To provide support and resolve disputes.
  • To run offers such as first-ride discounts, invites and Flytte Rewards.
  • To improve Flytte, using totals and trends rather than identifying individuals where we can.

We use your data on the basis of our contract with you (to provide the service), legal obligations (such as tax and accounting records), our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe, and your consent where we ask for it (for example, location access or notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time in your phone’s settings.

4. Who we share it with

  • Riders and drivers on a trip: riders see the driver’s first name, photo, vehicle, plate and rating; drivers see the rider’s first name, pickup and drop-off. Phone contact is only for that trip.
  • People you choose: anyone you share a live trip link with, and your emergency contact if you raise an SOS.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract: hosting, maps, messaging (SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram for sign-in codes), payments (Mobile Money and card processors) and weather data for pricing.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone’s life or safety.

We never sell personal data. Some providers may process data outside Uganda; when they do, we use providers with adequate protection, as the Act requires.

5. Location

Riders: we use your location to set your pickup, find nearby drivers and show your trip. Drivers: we use your location while you’re online and during trips to send you requests and show riders where you are. We don’t track your location when you’re offline and not on a trip.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is open.
  • Trip and payment records: as long as Ugandan tax and accounting law requires, after which we delete or anonymise them.
  • Driver documents: while you drive with Flytte, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes: a few minutes.

When you delete your account, we erase your name, number, documents, saved places and contacts straight away. Trips and payments stay only as anonymous records.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act you can ask to access your personal data, correct it, object to some uses, and have it deleted. You can update most details in the app and delete your account in Account → Settings → Delete my account. For anything else, contact us (section 11). If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda.

8. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, hashed sign-in codes, limited staff access and regular review. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Children

You must be 18 or older to create a Flytte account. Riders may book trips for others, including minors travelling with consent of a parent or guardian, but the account holder is responsible for that trip.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are important, we’ll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: use Help in the app or call Flytte support. We reply within 30 days.

Delete your account

For riders and drivers, with or without the app

What happens
  • Your name, phone number, email, saved places, emergency contact, photos and driver documents are erased straight away.
  • Trips and payments stay only as anonymous records for as long as Ugandan tax and accounting law requires.
  • Money in your Flytte Wallet is lost, so spend it first. Drivers must cash out or settle their balance first.
  • You can’t undo this. To ride or drive again, create a new account.

In the app: Account → Settings → Delete my account. No app? Confirm your number below.

Your account is deleted. We’ve erased your personal data. Thank you for riding with Flytte.

Can’t receive codes on that number any more? Email support@flytte.co from the email on your account, or see help and support. We reply within 30 days. More in our privacy policy.

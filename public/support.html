Help and support

Flytte rides, deliveries and Flytte Driver, in Kampala

Contact us
  • In the app: open your trip, then Help. It’s the fastest way, and we can see the trip.
  • Phone: see the app (Account → Help)
  • Email: support@flytte.co
  • In an emergency during a trip, use SOS in the app or call 999 or 112.

Riders

Booking. Tap Where to?, choose a ride and confirm your pickup. You see the price before you book and pay that price unless the trip changes, for example a new stop.

Paying. Cash, MTN or Airtel Mobile Money, card, or your Flytte Wallet. Receipts are in Activity, and by email if you add one.

Lost something? Open the trip in Activity and tap Help → I lost an item. We’ll put you in touch with the driver.

Charged wrongly, or a problem with a driver? Open the trip in Activity → Help and tell us what happened. We reply in the app.

Safety. Every trip has a 4-digit PIN to start, a live link you can share, SOS, and an emergency contact. You can ask for a woman driver.

Drivers

Signing up. Install Flytte Driver, add your vehicle and documents, and our team reviews them before your first trip.

Getting paid. You keep 88% of every fare. Cash out to Mobile Money from Earnings. On cash trips our commission comes off your balance.

Ride alerts. Turn on alerts in the app so requests reach you when your screen is off. Allow location “Always” so riders can see you while you’re online.

Your account

Change your details in Account. Delete your account in Account → Settings, or without the app. Read our privacy policy and terms.
